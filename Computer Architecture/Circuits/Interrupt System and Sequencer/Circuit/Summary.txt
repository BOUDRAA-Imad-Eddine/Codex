Part A : It is a classical hierachical IT system at system start the inhibition register Lvl 0 ,Lvl1(4 priority disks),Lvl2(4 rooms out of 6) , we also put the mask register of ou three levels to one and Validation FlipFlop to 1, we also design a priority encoder that return the highest priority IT level that is currently on

Part B : we designed a Daisy Chain implementing cascading cells , each is a flip flop and has it’s own Ack IN and out such that the Ack IN of a cell i is the Ack out of the cell i-1 Ack In of the whole daisy chain is the ack in of cell 1 and Ack Out of the last one is the Ack OUT signal 

to connect the daisy chain to our IT system from Part A we connect the IT signals generated by level 1 to the corresponding inpunt in the cells of the daisy chain which uses also a priority encoder to generate the highest priority controller bu sending the Ack signal

Part C : we implement our sequencer that generates 4 phases and 4 pulses which is controlled by enabling signal (IT level from part A should be 1) at phase 2 the Ack signal is sent into the daisy chain that return to us the device number, then the ROM we implemented returns to us the address of the routine (also controlled by phase 3), at phase 4 we write our address onto the PC
